# SEO audit — Accountability Guild (2026-07-13)

## Method and what this audit can and can't claim

Two data sources were unavailable this session: Ahrefs and Semrush are both connected but return "insufficient plan" for keyword volume, rankings, backlinks, and automated site-audit crawls (Ahrefs' free domain-rating check did work — see Finding 1). Everything below instead comes from directly fetching the live site (metadata, HTML, structured data, robots.txt, sitemap.xml) and a handful of manual web searches to gauge competitive crowding. The search spot-checks are directional signal, not verified rank tracking — treat them as "here's what a person searching this term sees today," not a keyword report. Real rank/volume/backlink data should come from Google Search Console (the repo already documents a GSC setup — see `docs/marketing/seo-founding-articles-baseline-2026-04-12.md`) or an Ahrefs/Semrush plan tier that includes API access.

## Finding 1 (headline): the site has no measurable search footprint yet

- Ahrefs' free Domain Rating check returns **0.0** — no backlinks in Ahrefs' index. Expected for a new public-beta domain, but worth stating plainly.
- A search for `site:accountabilityguild.com` returned zero results from the domain itself — only unrelated sites with "accountability" in the name.
- A branded search for `"Accountability Guild" accountabilityguild.com` also did not surface the site at all, even in a list otherwise full of tangential "accountability" results (including a WoW guild).
- Nothing in robots.txt blocks standard search crawling (`Content-Signal: search=yes`, `Allow: /` for `User-agent: *`), so this isn't a robots misconfiguration — it reads as a new, low-authority domain that likely hasn't been meaningfully crawled/indexed yet, or has been crawled but not indexed for lack of any authority signal.
- **This is the priority fix, ahead of content strategy.** No amount of new articles matters if the domain isn't indexed. Concrete next steps: submit the sitemap in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools if not already done, request indexing on the homepage and 2-3 key pages, and pursue a small number of easy early citations/backlinks (a relevant directory turned up in search results during this audit: `thehiveindex.com/topics/accountability/` — "18 Best Accountability Communities" — worth a listing submission; Product Hunt launch is another standard move for a new public-beta SaaS tool).

## Technical SEO: solid foundation, one real gap

What's working well, confirmed directly on the homepage, `/pricing`, `/how-it-works`, `/resources`, and multiple `/resources/[slug]` pages:
- Unique, well-formed `<title>` and meta description on every page checked — no duplication, reasonable lengths, descriptive.
- Consistent canonical tags, matching Open Graph and Twitter Card metadata, `viewport` tag present, `lang="en"` set — no mobile-rendering or duplicate-content red flags.
- Structured data (JSON-LD) is present and valid: `Organization` and `WebSite` (with `SearchAction`) schema on the homepage, plus `Article` schema (headline, description, datePublished, dateModified) on resource articles.
- `sitemap.xml` is live, referenced correctly from `robots.txt`, and lists all 17 public pages (4 core pages + 13 resource articles) with sensible `priority` values.
- Single `<h1>` per page, in every page checked.

The one technical gap worth fixing:

**robots.txt blocks every major AI crawler.** The Cloudflare-managed block list explicitly disallows `GPTBot`, `ClaudeBot`, `Google-Extended`, `Applebot-Extended`, `Amazonbot`, `Bytespider`, `CCBot`, and `meta-externalagent`, and sets `Content-Signal: ai-train=no`. Regular search engine crawling (Googlebot, Bingbot) is unaffected — `search=yes` and blanket `Allow: /` are set for `User-agent: *`. But this means the site is opting out of being cited in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini/AI Overviews, and similar AI-answer surfaces, which is an increasingly real discovery channel for exactly the kind of comparison and how-to queries this content targets ("accountability partner app with money stakes," "goal setting guide," etc.). Worth a deliberate decision rather than a default: if the intent is genuinely "don't let AI train on our content," keep it; if it was inherited from a Cloudflare default and not a considered choice, it's blocking free distribution for a pre-revenue product that needs discovery more than it needs content protection.

Not checked this session (need PageSpeed Insights API access or a Lighthouse run, not available via the tools on hand): Core Web Vitals, load performance, image optimization at the CDN level. Recommend a Lighthouse/PageSpeed pass as a fast follow.

## Content architecture: 13 articles, split between two very different competitive realities

The resource library breaks cleanly into two groups, and they face opposite odds:

**Articles 1-7 (goal-setting, types-of-personal-goals, personal-accountability, behavior-change, personal-life-audit, life-planning, self-improvement)** target broad, generic self-improvement queries. A spot-check on `"goal setting guide"` returned a page dominated by James Clear, Brian Tracy, Mark Manson, NSLS, and Jack Canfield — all high-authority sites with years of backlinks. Against Ahrefs DR 0, these seven articles are extremely unlikely to rank for their target terms regardless of on-page quality. They're also the weakest fit for the group-owner audience the product actually serves.

**Articles 8-13 (startup-accountability-system, proof-entries, designing-consequences, weekly-reporting-template, 30-day-sprint, resource-library-building)** target narrower, more product-adjacent queries tied to running a real accountability cycle. A spot-check on `"accountability partner app with money stakes"` surfaced direct competitors — StickK, Beeminder, Forfeit, Accountablo, AccountabiliBuddy — but all of those are solo commitment-contract tools; a separate check on `"accountability group software for friends"` surfaced Habitat, HabitShare, Squad, Folksable, Cohorty — group habit trackers, but none with real financial stakes or a pooled group kitty. **The specific combination Accountability Guild occupies — multi-person group, recurring cycle, pooled kitty, receipts-based reporting — doesn't have an obvious direct competitor in either search.** That's a genuine whitespace angle worth leaning into in content, rather than competing head-on in the crowded generic self-improvement space.

Other content-level findings:
- **Article #13, "Building A Resource Library That Turns Product Interest Into Qualified Signups,"** is written for marketers/content strategists about content strategy itself — not a topic the actual buyer (someone starting or running an accountability group) would ever search for. It reads like an internal planning doc that got published to the public resource library. Worth reconsidering whether this belongs in `/resources` at all, versus being an internal doc.
- **Read-time estimates don't match content length.** The goal-setting article displays "14 min read" but measures at 961 words — closer to a 4-5 minute read. If this pattern holds across the other articles (worth a full sweep), it's a small trust/UX issue and can also signal thin content relative to what competitors publish on the same terms.
- **No images anywhere on the site** — zero `<img>` tags found on the homepage or any article checked. No hero images, diagrams, screenshots, or charts. This isn't a technical SEO problem (no missing alt text, because there's nothing to caption) but it is a content-depth and engagement gap against competitors who use screenshots, comparison tables, and diagrams — especially relevant for `/resources` articles trying to out-teach incumbents.
- **No FAQ schema despite FAQ content.** The goal-setting article has a visible, on-page FAQ section but no `FAQPage` JSON-LD — a missed opportunity for FAQ rich snippets in search results.
- **All 13 articles are dated April 10-16, 2026** (per both visible bylines and `datePublished`/`dateModified` in schema) with no updates since — a three-month-old batch with no evidence of an ongoing publishing cadence. `dateModified` equals `datePublished` on every article checked, meaning nothing has been revised since launch.
- **Internal "next article" links follow publish order, not topic.** On some articles this works (the founder-playbook article links to proof-entries, consequences, and reporting-template — topically tight). On others it doesn't ("Building a Resource Library" — the marketer-facing piece — links next to Goal Setting, Types of Personal Goals, and Personal Accountability, which have nothing to do with it). A topic-based "related articles" module (already present inconsistently — the goal-setting article has one, the founder and resource-library articles don't) would be a stronger, cheap fix.

## Prioritized recommendations

1. **Get the site indexed.** Submit sitemap + request indexing in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools; pursue 2-3 easy early backlinks/citations (accountability-tool directories, Product Hunt). Nothing else on this list matters until this is fixed.
2. **Decide deliberately on the AI-crawler block in robots.txt.** Keep it only if content protection is genuinely the priority over AI-driven discovery.
3. **Reallocate content investment away from articles 1-7's generic terms and toward the money-stakes-group whitespace** (articles 8-13's territory) where there's no direct competitor doing the same thing.
4. **Pull or repurpose article #13** ("Building a Resource Library...") out of the public-facing library, or clearly reframe it for a buyer-relevant angle.
5. **Add FAQPage schema** to articles that already have on-page FAQ sections (cheap, direct rich-snippet upside).
6. **Audit read-time labels against actual word counts** across all 13 articles and correct or lengthen content to match.
7. **Add a topic-based "related articles" module consistently** across all articles, replacing publish-order-based "next" links where they don't make topical sense.
8. **Run a Lighthouse/PageSpeed pass** once tool access allows, to close out the Core Web Vitals blind spot.
9. **Connect Google Search Console access to this workspace** (or confirm it's queryable another way) so future audits use real impression/click/position data instead of manual spot-checks.
