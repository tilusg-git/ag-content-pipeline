How to read this

Burning pain: the problem hurts now and the current workaround is bad. Willingness to pay: they already spend money or stake money on this. Winnable share: we can become the default for this segment against what they use today. Referral potential: members talk to each other, and the audience contains the next customers.

Weighted score = Σ(weight × score) ÷ Σ(weights), on the 1 to 5 scale. The small arrow under each rank compares it with the default (equal weights, original scores).

What the evidence can't tell us

No segment has interview data from a group owner outside Brandon's own group. Pain and willingness to pay are inferred from adjacent spending (dues, bets, subscriptions), not from asking. The business evaluation's gate still applies: 10 group-owner interviews, then 5 groups through 3 cycles.

Which act does nobody cover well?

Pooling the forfeits. Only DietBet and StepBet pool money, and only for fitness games WayBetter runs, with the pot split among winners. Venmo Groups and Splitwise settle who owes whom, but nothing links a payment to a missed commitment. Close with receipts is a close second. The tools that close fully are individual stakes apps (stickK, Beeminder, Forfeit). Group closes everywhere else happen live, with a leader going through a sheet or question list. EO's forum update is the formal version.

Which free substitute is hardest to displace?

The group chat. It is already installed, it is where the group talks, and it loosely covers three acts: people post goals, drop proof photos and report at the end of the week. We shouldn't try to replace it. The product should post into it (reminders, close summaries, kitty totals) so the chat becomes a way in. The shared Google Sheet comes second for owner-led groups, because the owner built it and controls it.

Did any segment's ranking change?

Yes. Two Winnable share scores were lowered on 1 Nov 2026. With equal weights, church groups fall from tied 3rd to tied 4th with fitness (3.50 → 3.25), because Planning Center already sits in 78,000+ churches. Group coaches fall to 7th on their own (2.75 → 2.50), because CoachAccountable is a dedicated accountability tool for coaches. The top two stay tied, and solo founders are now 3rd on their own.
