# Video ad structure: reference for AG video work

*Saved 2026-11-02 from a "How to Structure Any B2B Video AD" cheat sheet Grant shared (a LinkedIn-style post; author not recorded). The original chart is next to this file: [`b2b-video-ad-structure-cheatsheet.png`](b2b-video-ad-structure-cheatsheet.png). The tables below transcribe it; the AG notes at the end are ours. Read this before scripting any video: homepage explainer, paid social video, launch video, or testimonial.*

## The core idea

Pick the structure before the creative. Each video length has a different job, and each job has a beat sequence that works. The creative goes on top of the sequence, and the sequence keeps it from wandering.

- **6 s: one quick message.** Usually unskippable pre-roll. One beat (a pain, a use case, a value prop) plus a CTA. Room for about 6 to 8 words.
- **15 s: a tight story.** Enough time for a flow: hook, pain, product, outcome, CTA. Many of the best-performing paid ads are this length.
- **30 s: show a use case.** Room for a character or a situation. Get the brand name in early in case the viewer skips.
- **60 s launch: a bigger moment.** Launches and announcements. Go bigger on creative; the structure keeps it on track. The post claims 60 s ads often do better on LinkedIn than on YouTube (unverified).
- **Testimonials: let customers sell.** Open on a real outcome or pain, then add proof and context. Keep it grounded in one real use case.

Timing failures to avoid: no hook and you lose them; product too early and you confuse them; a weak CTA wastes the attention you earned.

## Beat types

| Beat | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Hook | Attention grab in the first seconds |
| Pain | The problem the viewer has |
| Brand | Name, logo, who we are |
| Comp. Alt | The competitor or current alternative (contrast) |
| Feature | What the product does, shown |
| Benefit | What the viewer gets |
| CTA | The action to take |
| Social Proof | Customers, logos, quotes, numbers |
| Offer | Price, deal, or reason to act now |

## Sequences (read left to right, roughly equal segments unless noted)

### Paid ads, 6 s
| Name | Sequence |
|---|---|
| Quick use case hit | Feature (0-5 s) → CTA |
| Agitate pain, provoke curiosity | Pain (0-5 s) → CTA |
| Trusted by | Social proof (0-5 s) → CTA |
| Leave an impression | Brand (0-5 s) → CTA |
| Conversion ad (use sparingly) | CTA only |

### Paid ads, 15 s
| Name | Sequence |
|---|---|
| Classic software ad | Pain → Feature → Benefit → CTA |
| Positioning ad | Comp. alt → Feature → Benefit |
| Benefit-focused software ad | Brand → Benefit → Feature → CTA |
| Use case ad | Feature (long) → Benefit (long) → CTA |
| Brand ad (vibes) | Brand → Benefit → CTA |

### Paid ads, 30 s
| Name | Sequence |
|---|---|
| Classic software ad | Pain → Brand → Feature → Benefit → CTA |
| Positioning ad | Comp. alt → Brand → Feature → Benefit → CTA |
| Feel-good ad | Benefit → Brand → Feature → Benefit → Feature (short) → CTA |
| Quick overview ad | Benefit → Pain → Feature → Pain → Feature → CTA |
| Brand ad (vibes with product) | Benefit → Feature → Brand → CTA |

### Launch videos, 60 s
| Name | Sequence |
|---|---|
| Product explainer ad | Hook → Pain → Feature → Benefit → Pain → Feature → Benefit → CTA |
| Product explainer (homepage) | Hook → Feature → Brand → Feature → Benefit → CTA |
| Product launch | Hook → Brand → Benefit → Feature → Social proof |
| Product launch (offer forward) | Hook → Pain → Offer → Brand → Feature → Social proof → CTA |
| Product launch, positioning | Comp. alt → Brand → Feature → Social proof → CTA |
| Funding announcement | Brand → Pain → Brand → Feature → Benefit → Social proof |

### Customer ads
| Length | Name | Sequence |
|---|---|---|
| 15 s | Testimonial with pain | Brand → Pain → Social proof → CTA |
| 30 s | Testimonial | Social proof → Hook → Brand → Pain → Feature → CTA |
| 60 s | Long-form | Pain → Hook → Social proof → Feature → Brand → CTA |

## How this applies to Accountability Guild

- **No humor.** The cheat sheet treats 30 s as the place for a joke. Voice-and-tone rules that out (fully straight). Use a situation or a character instead: a group that meets, a promise that fades, a report at the close.
- **Social proof: don't invent it.** AG is in public beta with no customer testimonials, logos, or usage numbers to cite. Skip the Social Proof beat (and every testimonial format) until real members agree to appear. The honest stand-in is the founder story: Brandon built it for his own first-Thursday group. Research stats from the behavior-science KB (✓ entries only) can fill a proof beat, but they are not social proof and must not be framed as customer results.
- **Our Comp. alt is the group chat and the spreadsheet,** not a named competitor. Reddit ad A ("the story": spreadsheet vs. cycle report) is already a positioning ad in still form. stickK, Beeminder, and Coach.me are solo tools; contrast on "your group" rather than naming them.
- **Our Offer beat is the price:** no monthly fee; $1 platform fee plus card processing only when a forfeit is charged. The money staying with the group (the kitty) is the Benefit beat.
- **Brand early for anything 30 s or longer** that can be skipped.
- **Captions burned in** on every video; most feeds and the homepage play muted.
- **Glossary terms on screen and in narration:** "commitment" not "contract", "kitty" not "group fund". Question headlines are allowed in paid social only.
- **The homepage explainer** (`docs/marketing/homepage-explainer-video-plan-2026-10-19.md`, 90 s, with Brandon) already follows the "Product explainer (homepage)" shape, with a pain opening and an Offer beat (pricing) before the CTA: Pain → Brand → Feature × 4 acts → Benefit (kitty) → Offer → CTA. No change needed; use it as the reference build.
- **Cut-downs to make from the explainer footage** once it exists: a 15 s "Classic software ad" (pain: promises fade → feature: mark the week → benefit: the kitty → CTA) and a 6 s "Agitate pain" (*Promises fade between meetings.* → Create your group) for paid video tests.
