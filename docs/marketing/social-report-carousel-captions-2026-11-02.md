## Option A: one carousel post (Facebook + Instagram)

> Every cycle ends with a report. This is what filing one looks like.
>
> 1. The header keeps the score: what you kept, what it cost, and what is still unreported. In this example, 4 of 5 kept and a $10 forfeit.
> 2. Your marks from the month sit above each commitment as evidence. They inform the score. They don't set it.
> 3. You choose the score and write a sentence or two on what happened. Half done is an honest answer, and the report has room for it.
> 4. Add a reflection and file. The forfeit pools into your group's kitty, never to the company.
>
> Your group reads every report at the close. That is the part that changes the next cycle.
>
> What does your group's end-of-month check-in look like right now?
>
> #Accountability #AccountabilityGroup #FollowThrough
