## Headline finding: the differentiated language has ~no measurable search volume

Hyper-specific AG phrases — "accountability kitty," "group accountability app with money," "pay for missed goals app," "financial stakes goal app," "accountability app with money" — returned **zero or unrecorded volume** in Semrush. That's not a tooling gap; it confirms the SEO audit's whitespace finding literally: nobody is searching in AG's own product language yet. The real, measurable volume sits on **broad, generic accountability terms**. The strategy this implies: rank content on the broad head terms below, and use them as the vehicle to introduce the group + money-stakes + kitty mechanic as the differentiator *within* the piece — not as the search target itself.

Also flagging a data-quality issue: Semrush's `phrase_questions` report returned mostly irrelevant results for this niche (substring/stem collisions like "how to delete cash app account," "what is partner capital account" — matching on "account"/"partner," not the accountability concept). **Don't trust that tool's PAA output for this topic** — the FAQ sections in the briefs below are proposed from judgment and the existing product docs, not from a clean PAA pull. Flagging so nobody assumes these were machine-sourced questions.
