# Quote Bank — Social Content

Curated quotes for Accountability Guild social posts, Field notes pull-quotes, and marketing copy. Companion to `behavior-science-knowledge-base.md` (stats and studies live there; quotes live here).

**Provenance:** Compiled via Gemini deep research (2026-07-20), then attribution-reviewed and curated by Claude on 2026-07-20. The raw Gemini output had 415 entries with an **empty citation column**, ~40 fabricated "James Clear" quotes, and a dozen famous misattributions — see [Cut list](#cut-list-removed-quotes) for what was removed and why. Original raw output not retained; this file is the source of record.

---

## Instructions for Claude (read this first)

**Rules:**

1. **Only use ✓ quotes verbatim with attribution in published content.** ⚠ = standard attribution but no verified primary source — post only with "attributed to" phrasing, or verify first. ✗ quotes appear only in the Cut list and must never be published.
2. **Use the exact wording given here.** Several entries were corrected from Gemini's paraphrases back to the real published wording (marked "wording corrected"). Don't re-paraphrase.
3. **No science claims from this file.** The Gemini source dressed quotes in unsourced neuroscience ("anterior cingulate," "basal ganglia consolidation," ego depletion). All research claims must come from `behavior-science-knowledge-base.md` — including its ban on "willpower is a muscle" framing.
4. **Attribution format for social:** em-dash + name, e.g. `— Jim Rohn`. For living authors quoting their own books, name + book on first use in an article (e.g. "James Clear, *Atomic Habits*").
5. **Match quote to pillar.** AG's voice is group + stakes + follow-through, not generic hustle. Prefer quotes about being answerable *to others*, visible effort, and systems over quotes about lone-wolf grind.
6. **Don't stack quotes.** One quote per post, paired with an AG mechanic or a stat from the behavior-science KB.
7. **Retire a quote once it's posted.** When a quote from this file goes live in published content (social post, Field notes pull-quote, ad), move it — in the same task that ships the post — out of every active table it appears in, **including Top shelf**, and into the [Posted — retired from rotation](#posted--retired-from-rotation) log below, recording the channel and date. This stops the same quote from being posted twice and keeps future research runs from re-surfacing it. Retiring is mandatory, not optional cleanup.

**Flag key:** ✓ verified or standard attribution to the author's own published work · ⚠ widely attributed, primary source unverified · ✗ known misattribution or fabrication (Cut list only)
