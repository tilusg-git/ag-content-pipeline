# Brandon's bookshelf — source library for social quotes and content ideation

Created 2026-09-19 from two shelf photos Brandon uploaded. Titles/authors were read off the spines; anything I could not read is flagged. This is a **source library**, not a quote bank: no verbatim quotes live here.

## How this feeds social content (workflow)

1. Claude proposes a book + passage/idea from the "Opportunities" section below.
2. Brandon highlights the passage in his physical copy, photographs the page, and uploads it.
3. Claude reads the quote from the photo. A quote confirmed against the physical page counts as **✓ verified** (record book, author, page number). This sidesteps the misattribution problem documented in `docs/research/quote-bank.md`.
4. Card is built per `docs/brand/image-backgrounds-spec.md` (the photo of the highlighted page can serve as the image source; flat ink scrim rules apply).
5. On post, log the quote in the "Posted — retired from rotation" section of `docs/research/quote-bank.md` (rule 7) with channel + date, and mark it used in the table below.

Rules: short excerpts only, always attribute (author, title). Stats and science claims still come from `behavior-science-knowledge-base.md`, not from these books, unless the book's own claim is verified in the photo and framed as that author's claim. Voice per `docs/brand/voice-and-tone.md` (straight, no humor, no emoji).

## Opportunities (ranked for AG)

Passages below are **themes and well-known ideas to hunt for**, not quotes. Do not publish any wording from this list without the page photo.

### Tier 1 — build cards around these first

- **Radical Candor (Kim Scott).** The "care personally, challenge directly" framework and its four quadrants (radical candor, ruinous empathy, obnoxious aggression, manipulative insincerity). Direct fit to peer accountability: a group that is nice but never names a miss is practicing ruinous empathy. Strongest single source for a "why groups fail" post.
- **The Dichotomy of Leadership (Willink & Babin).** Built on paired tensions (own it all but empower others; resolute but not overbearing; train hard but train smart, etc.). Good for "owner-led vs. peer" framing and for forfeit-amount balance. Note Willink is already in the rotation (Willink card in recent commits); check the quote bank for the source book before duplicating.
- **Leadership Strategy and Tactics: Field Manual (Willink).** Short, punchy, standalone lines. Best raw material for one-line cards. Highlight anything on discipline, ownership, or standards.
- **High Output Management (Grove).** Origin of OKR thinking; ideas on pairing indicators (quantity plus quality), measuring output rather than activity, and regular one-on-ones. Maps directly to "measurable commitments" and "close with receipts." Strong for Field notes citations (founder/execution audience).
- **Tribe of Mentors (Ferris).** 100+ short mentor answers. Highest quote density on the shelf. Attribute each quote to the person who said it ("quoted in Tribe of Mentors"), not to Ferris. Good for a recurring series.

### Tier 2 — strong when the topic matches

- **Primed to Perform (Doshi & McGregor).** Their "total motivation" model (play, purpose, potential vs. emotional pressure, economic pressure, inertia). **Caution:** the book argues that economic pressure can erode performance, which is in tension with AG's financial forfeit. Do not cherry-pick. This is worth a deliberate Field notes piece that engages it honestly (forfeit as a group-agreed stake tied to purpose, not an employer's carrot/stick), and it should be vetted before any card quotes it.
- **The Mentally Strong Leader (Mautz).** Habits of resilient leaders; good for consistency and follow-through posts. Read the highlighted passage before committing.
- **Slack (DeMarco).** Argument that running at 100% utilization removes the capacity to change. Angle: why hall passes exist and why cadence beats intensity.
- **Experimentation Works (Thomke).** Test-and-learn culture. Angle: treat a cycle's commitments as experiments; misses are data.
- **Hold'em Wisdom for All Players (Negreanu).** Money on the line changes decisions. Angle: stakes, risk, and decision quality. Keep clear of anything reading as gambling promotion (financial-services ad-review risk noted in the Reddit plan).
- **Get to the Point! (Schwartzberg).** Clarity of message. Angle: writing a commitment so short and specific it can be graded.

### Tier 3 — occasional or niche

- **StrengthsFinder 2.0 (Rath):** strengths-based framing; low tie to follow-through.
- **Don't Sweat the Small Stuff at Work (Carlson):** calm/perspective; off-tone for a semi-blunt brand unless a line is very sharp.
- **Financial Intelligence (Berman & Knight):** numbers-literacy angle; possible for kitty/pricing transparency posts.
- **The Resolution for Men Bible Study:** only relevant for the Christian-accountability-group keyword thread in the blog target sheet. Scripture-derived lines would need separate handling. Brandon's call whether this is on-brand.

### Skip

- **ADHD Does Not Exist:** contested medical claim; do not quote it for marketing.
- **Dad's Book of Awesome Projects, The Love Fix, Proactive Selling, Fanchise Your Franchise:** off-topic for AG content.
