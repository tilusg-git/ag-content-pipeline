When you can't see far, commit short. One cycle. One promise. Visible to the people who'll ask.

Most plans get blurry a few weeks out. That's not a failure of nerve — it's just how distance works.

You don't need to see the whole route. You need a horizon short enough to stand behind: one cycle, one measurable commitment, logged where your group can see it.

The fog won't lift on your schedule. The cycle still closes on schedule. Someone still checks the receipts.

Keep going — not because the end is in view, but because this cycle is, and the people you owe it to are watching.
