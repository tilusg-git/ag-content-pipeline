`Tooltip` wraps a dotted-underline trigger with a small ink tooltip on hover/focus. `Popover` is a larger free-standing ink-bordered card for richer explanations.

```jsx
<Tooltip label="Charged to the card on file">Forfeit</Tooltip>
<Popover heading="What's a hall-pass?">One forgiven miss per cycle, toggled by the group owner.</Popover>
```
